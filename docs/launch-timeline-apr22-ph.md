## Engagement principles

- **Reply, don't thank.** "Thanks for the support!" is filler. "That's a great question — the reason Impact gets 2x weight over Effort is because most people I interviewed underweighted how much execution pain compounds" is a reply.
- **Don't push PH in every channel simultaneously.** The people who follow you across X + Threads + LinkedIn get tired of the same post 3x. Vary tone per channel.
- **Never DM a stranger "please upvote."** Ever. Only personal asks to real contacts.
- **Reciprocal upvotes:** fine, but only for products you genuinely find interesting. PH moderators can detect vote rings; weighted voting downweights transactional votes anyway.

## Contingency

- **PH goes live but product page has wrong data (gallery order, tagline, etc.):** fix via PH edit UI ASAP. Cosmetic fixes during the day are allowed; content fixes should happen in the first 30 min before traffic builds.
- **Low upvote velocity by 18:00 WIT:** send 5 more personalized DMs. Do NOT mass DM.

- **App Store page has "downloading now" spike but PH is flat:** your App Store tweet is outperforming PH. Pin the PH link in your X bio. Do not panic. Different audiences.
