## Nomination Description (copy this)

```
IdeaTamer is a gamified idea focus app built exclusively for iOS 26 with SwiftUI and SwiftData. It helps makers and creators stop collecting ideas and start finishing them.

The core loop: Capture ideas → Score them on Impact, Effort, and Alignment (1–100) → Activate your top idea as a Quest with milestones → Ship it to your Hall of Fame. Only one quest can be active at a time — this intentional constraint is the feature.

What makes it unique: Weekly Duel lets you compete against your past self across four rounds (XP, milestones, quests shipped, streak days). Full gamification with XP, levels (Spark → Legend), streaks, and badges — turning productivity into a game against yourself.

Built with zero third-party dependencies — pure Swift, SwiftUI, SwiftData, SF Symbols, and Local Notifications. Full Dark Mode support. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no network requests. All data stays on-device. Free to download, no ads.

We are flexible on publish timing and happy to coordinate with Apple's editorial calendar.

Website: https://ideatamer.app
Contact: ideatamerapp@gmail.com
```
