## Friday — Pick 1 of 3

### Option A

finally shipped a side project after mass-quitting every project I've started for the past two years.

what actually changed:

• scored every idea 1-100 instead of just vibing about which one "felt right"
• picked the top score. didn't let myself touch anything else.
• broke it into tiny milestones (like embarrassingly small)
• set up a weekly thing where I compete against my own stats from last week

the scoring was the unlock tbh. once I had a number I stopped second-guessing.

### Option B

six months ago:
• 30+ ideas in Apple Notes
• 4 half-started Xcode projects
• zero apps on the App Store
• guilt. lots of guilt.

now:
• every idea has a score
• one project at a time (non-negotiable)
• competing with my own stats weekly
• actually finishing things??

the only thing that changed was I stopped letting myself work on more than one idea. that's it.

### Option C

I think your notes app is making you worse at finishing things.

every time you dump an idea in there without evaluating it, you're adding to a pile that makes it harder to pick one. the pile grows. picking gets harder. so you add another idea instead of starting one. the cycle repeats.

I capped myself at 10 unscored ideas. had to rate each one before adding another. forced decision-making. felt uncomfortable. worked.

## Saturday — Thread (6 posts)

### Post 1
I built an app from Ambon, Indonesia. population 350k. no startup scene. no dev meetups. the nearest Apple Store is a 4-hour flight away.

five things I figured out building solo:

### Post 2
1. not having money made the product better

couldn't afford servers → built it offline-first
couldn't hire a designer → kept the UI dead simple
couldn't maintain complex dependencies → used zero third-party libraries

I used to see these as problems. now they're features that people actually want.

### Post 3
2. the feature everyone pushes back on is the one that matters

"wait, I can only work on ONE idea at a time?"

yes. that's literally why the app exists. if you could work on everything at once you'd finish nothing. I know because that's what I did for two years.

### Post 4
3. gamification gets a bad rap but streaks are no joke

XP and levels sound silly until you're on day 8 of a streak and you genuinely don't want to break it. the weekly duel thing (you vs. your stats from 7 days ago) works because losing to yourself is weirdly motivating.

### Post 5
4. not collecting data is a feature

no accounts. no cloud. no analytics. when I tell people this they assume the app is unfinished. nah. I just think your ideas are none of my business. or anyone else's.

### Post 6
5. the hardest part of building solo isn't the code

it's deciding the thing is ready. I could add features for another year. but the core loop works and the longer I wait the more I'm just procrastinating.

IdeaTamer launches April 13. ideatamer.app
